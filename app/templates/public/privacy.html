Privacy Policy

Client-RaQ stores account data, subscription records, and message metadata required to operate your workspace. Each company’s data is isolated by company_id.

WhatsApp and Telegram deliver message content under their policies; we process it to power your assistant and admin inbox.

Payment card data for platform subscriptions is handled by Paystack — we store references and amounts, not full card numbers.

Contact support via the channels published on clientraq.com for data requests.
