Quick map

How the bot uses your prices

  • Minimum quantity — set on each service. Below that, the bot warns the customer (important for small inch stickers on large-format machines).
  • Per sq ft / sq m — bot asks inches vs feet before calculating area × your rate.
  • Sizes / options — use for frames (5x7, 8x10…) or nylon tiers (50 pcs, 100 pcs with fixed prices). Bot prefers these over inventing a scale.
  • Payment — screenshots only pending until you confirm in Orders; then revenue updates.
